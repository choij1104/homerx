# HomeRx — Content rating (IARC questionnaire) and target audience

Play Console → App content → Content rating. Email for the IARC certificate: admin@hakoyallc.com.

## Category
**Reference, News, or Educational** (the app is a reference for OTC medicines and supplements).

| Is the app primarily a news app? | No | |

Expected result: a low age rating (e.g. ESRB Everyone / PEGI 3–12), possibly with a "drug
reference" descriptor depending on IARC. The store age rating does not set the target audience;
that is set separately below.

## Target audience and content (App content → Target audience)

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Target age groups | **18 and over** only |
| Could the store listing unintentionally appeal to children? | No |

is guidance for parents, not content for children).
