## Full description (≤4000)

```
HomeRx explains the over-the-counter (OTC) medicines and dietary supplements sold in U.S. pharmacies, in English, Korean (한국어) and Japanese (日本語).

It is made for immigrants, older adults, caregivers and students: anyone who stands in a pharmacy aisle and finds the labels hard to follow. HomeRx is an educational reference. It does not diagnose or prescribe, and it does not replace your pharmacist or doctor.

DRUG FACTS, TRANSLATED
• 81 entries: pain and fever, allergy, cold and cough, nose, eye and ear, stomach, constipation, motion sickness, skin, first aid, mouth, sleep, women's health, quitting smoking, vitamins, minerals and herbal supplements.
• Each medicine is summarized in the same order as the U.S. Drug Facts label: active ingredient, uses, warnings, directions, and when to stop and ask a doctor.
• Every entry lists its sources (DailyMed labels, NIH Office of Dietary Supplements, NCCIH, CDC) and the date it was checked.
• Supplements show the recommended amount, the upper limit, what the evidence says, and the medicines they interact with.

MY MEDICINE CABINET
• Add what you have at home. HomeRx warns when two products contain the same ingredient (for example acetaminophen twice), when medicines of the same kind are stacked (two NSAIDs, two drowsy antihistamines), and when a label warning matches the health conditions you ticked.
• Track expiry dates.

MEDICINE FROM KOREA AND JAPAN
• Look up a medicine you used at home (Tylenol 500, Geworin, Pancold, Loxonin S, EVE A, Gaster 10 and more) and see what is in it and whether the same ingredient is sold over the counter in the U.S.

LEARN AND ASK
• How to read a Drug Facts label, measuring liquid medicine, getting rid of old medicine, older adults and chronic conditions, supplement safety.
• Phrases to show your pharmacist in English, with your language below.

PRIVATE AND OFFLINE
No account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking. Your cabinet and settings stay on your phone. Works without an internet connection. Large text options.

SAFETY
Before first use HomeRx shows a short notice in your language. Emergency numbers are always one tap away: 911, Poison Control 1-800-222-1222, and the 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline.

IMPORTANT
HomeRx is educational material only. It is not medical advice, diagnosis or treatment and is not a medical device. Always read your product's label and ask a pharmacist or doctor before using a medicine or supplement, especially if you are pregnant or breastfeeding, giving medicine to a child, over 65, living with a chronic condition, or taking other medicines. In an emergency, call 911.

Made by HAKOYA LLC, Texas.
```
