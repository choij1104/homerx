## Full description (≤4000)

```
HomeRx helps you understand the over-the-counter (OTC) medicines and dietary supplements sold in U.S. pharmacies, in English, Korean (한국어) and Japanese (日本語).

It is written for immigrants, older adults, caregivers, students and community organizations: anyone who stands in a pharmacy aisle and finds the labels and ingredient names hard to follow. HomeRx is an educational reference. It does not diagnose, prescribe or replace your pharmacist or doctor; it helps you ask safer questions at the pharmacy.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• OTC medicines by symptom: cold and cough, allergy, pain and fever, stomach, skin, sleep, eye and ear, oral care, women's health, children, dizziness, gout, and first aid.
• For each medicine: the ingredient (generic) name, brand names you will see on U.S. shelves, what it is commonly used for, and what to check for safety before using it.
• Dietary supplements, each marked with how strong the evidence is, what people use it for, and the interactions and cautions to ask about.
• Older adults and chronic conditions: why first-generation antihistamines, decongestants, NSAIDs and some supplements need extra care with age, high blood pressure, blood thinners, and liver or kidney disease.
• Duplicate ingredients: how taking several cold, pain and sleep products together can double up acetaminophen, NSAIDs or antihistamines.
• When OTC is not enough: signs that mean call 911 now, and signs that mean see a doctor or pharmacist today.
• English phrases to show your pharmacist: hand over your phone with a ready-made question, shown in your language and in English.

EASY TO USE
• Search by symptom, ingredient or brand name (for example Tylenol, ibuprofen, allergy, melatonin).
• Filter by category and tap any card to open the details.
• Switch between English, Korean and Japanese at any time. Every entry is available in all three languages.
• Works fully offline. No account, no sign-up, no ads, no tracking. HomeRx does not collect or store any personal data.

SAFETY FIRST
Before you start, HomeRx shows a short disclaimer in your language and asks you to confirm that you understand it is educational material. Emergency numbers stay at the top of the screen: 911 for emergencies, Poison Control 1-800-222-1222, and 988 for a mental health crisis.

Reference standards named in the app: FDA OTC Monographs, DailyMed, MedlinePlus, NIH Office of Dietary Supplements, NIH LiverTox, CDC and Poison Control.

IMPORTANT
HomeRx is an educational reference only. It is not medical advice, diagnosis or treatment and is not a medical device. Always read the product label, and ask a licensed pharmacist or doctor before using any medicine or supplement, especially if you are pregnant or breastfeeding, giving medicine to a child, over 65, living with a chronic condition, or taking other medicines. In an emergency, call 911.

HomeRx is made by HAKOYA LLC, San Antonio, Texas.
```
