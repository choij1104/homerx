HomeRx Privacy Policy

Effective October 22, 2026. Publisher: HAKOYA LLC, Selma, Texas, USA. Contact: admin@hakoyallc.com

English

HomeRx does not collect, store on a server, sell, or share any personal information. It has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics, and no tracking.

The app works offline. The medicines you add to “My cabinet”, their expiry dates, the health conditions you tick in “My health”, your language and text-size choices are saved only on your own device (in the app's local storage). They never leave your device, and HAKOYA LLC cannot see them. Deleting the app, or clearing its data, removes them.

Links to source documents (for example DailyMed or the NIH) and phone numbers open in your browser or phone app; those services have their own privacy policies.

HomeRx is educational material, not medical advice, and is not intended for children.

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
